# Free Speech & Language Therapy Apps

Free digital speech and language therapy tools created by **Kaiting Tu Bronson, M.S., CCC-SLP**, a multilingual speech-language pathologist serving children, teens, and adults in Leander, Cedar Park, and North Austin, Texas.

## About These Tools

I created these free apps to make speech and language therapy resources more practical, flexible, and accessible.

As a multilingual SLP, I wanted tools that could be used across the languages I use in my work:

- English
- Spanish
- Mandarin Chinese
- Portuguese

These resources were created from my experience working with children and families, including autistic children and individuals who benefit from visual supports and communication tools.

## Available Apps

### Visual Schedule

A customizable visual schedule with:

- Pictures from your own device
- Activity labels
- Temporal vocabulary
- Check-off steps
- PDF export for sharing with families

Available in English, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, and Portuguese.

Use it here:
[Open Visual Schedule](visual_schedule.html)

---

### Token Reward Board

A flexible token board designed for real-time therapy sessions.

Features:

- Choose 1–10 trials
- Add a photo of the child's actual motivator
- Use across languages
- Create a personalized board on the fly

Available in English, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, and Portuguese.

Use it here:
[Open Token Reward Board](token_reward_board.html)
