Free Speech Therapy Tools That Work Offline!

Visual Schedule

Create a personalized visual schedule with pictures, activity labels, temporal vocabulary, and check-off steps. It’s available in English, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, and Portuguese.

You can take pictures directly from your phone or tablet, making the schedule concrete and meaningful to the child. You can also export it as a PDF to share with families, so if you’re a therapist, the visual support can carry over from your sessions into everyday life.

This tool can support children who benefit from visual supports for routines, transitions, communication, and participation, including many autistic children.

Token Reward Board

A customizable token reward board available in English, Spanish, Mandarin Chinese, and Portuguese.

Choose 1–10 stars to match the number of trials or activities you want to complete, and take a picture of the actual item, activity, or reward the child wants.

I created this because I didn’t want to rely on laminated reward boards with predetermined pictures. Sometimes you’re in the middle of a therapy session and discover that a child is motivated by something you didn’t anticipate. With this tool, you can take a quick picture and create a personalized board on the fly, right from your phone or tablet.

That means you can respond to the child’s motivation in the moment instead of having to go home, print something, laminate it, and bring it back next time. The goal is to make visual reinforcement more flexible and practical for real-life therapy.
